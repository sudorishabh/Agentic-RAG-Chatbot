Adaptation strategies:

· Replacing the vulnerable crops with potential crops in a phased manner

· Shifting in sowing windows/growing periods

· Change in cropping systems

· Growing only millets in drought prone regions

· Growing long rice in water logging area

· Farm level adaptations- change in timings, inputs crop grown etc

· Increasing the adaptive capacity of farmers

· Early warning/ forecasting systems

· Community watershed management
