# Let's Make a Solar Cooker!


## YOU WILL NEED

. a large cardboard box . thermocol . aluminium foil . clear plastic wrap
. a sheet of black paper . ruler . pair of scissors . glue . tape


<figure>
</figure>


1
Start by taking a cardboard box.
Leaving an even margin of 2 cm on
each side, draw a square on its lid.

2
Make a flap on the lid by cutting
three sides of the square. Fold this
flap out so that it stands up when
the lid is closed.


<figure>
</figure>


3
Take five one-inch thick pieces
of thermocol and stick them to
the base and walls of the box so
that they provide insulation.


<figure>
</figure>


4
Glue aluminium foil to all
the thermocol pieces. The
walls and base of the box
should now have an even layer of
aluminium foil on them.

5
Make the reflector by gluing
a piece of aluminium foil to
the inside of the flap.

6
Glue black paper to
the base of the box.

7
Use a sheet of glass or clear
plastic wrap to create an
airtight window for sunlight
to enter the box. Do this by
taping the glass or plastic over the
opening of the flap.


<figure>
</figure>


<figure>
</figure>


8
Open the lid carefully and place
your food inside. Set the flap
open in such a way that the foil
reflects sunlight and directs it inside.
Use a ruler or stick to support the
flap. Your solar cooker is now ready!
